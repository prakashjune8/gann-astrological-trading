# Skill 3 – Gann Market Harmonics

## Time factors (geocentric tropical longitudes)
* **Major aspects** (exact hits): 0° conjunction, 90°/270° square, 120°/240° trine, 180° opposition.
  Optional Gann 1/8 harmonics: 45°, 135°, 225°, 315°.
* **Ingresses**: sign changes every 30°; cardinal points (0° Aries/Cancer/Libra/Capricorn) are
  Gann's seasonal time points and are weighted higher.
* **Stations**: retrograde/direct turns of Mercury→Pluto (speed sign change).
* **Lunar phases**: New/Full/Quarter Moon = Sun–Moon 0/90/180/270.
* Weight by planet "mass": slow planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) and the Sun dominate;
  Gann's key cycles: Jupiter–Saturn (~20 y), Saturn–Uranus, Mars–Jupiter.

## Price ↔ time squaring
* **Square of Nine**: a degree d on the wheel corresponds to price P where `sqrt(P) mod 2 = d/180`,
  i.e. `P = (2k + d/180)^2`. Every planetary longitude yields a ladder of price levels; pick the rungs
  nearest the reference price (one rung above, one below).
* **Price → degrees**: `price mod 360` (Gann's natural square). A planet transiting that degree
  "squares price with time".
* **Time from pivot**: when the Sun has moved 90°, 120°, 180°, 240°, 270°, 360° from a pivot's solar
  longitude, time is squared (seasonal divisions of the year).
* Report triggers as *windows* (Gann allowed ±1–3 days), never as guaranteed reversals.
